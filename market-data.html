What memory care actually costs, state by state

A family comparing memory care communities is usually comparing two different prices at once: the advertised monthly rate and the bill after level-of-care charges. Public survey data puts the national middle of that range near $7,000 a month, with a gap of thousands of dollars a month between the cheapest and priciest states. The figures below separate the base rate from the add-ons and show where geography moves the total most.

The honest caveat: Genworth does not publish a separate state-by-state memory care median, so state memory care figures are compilations that apply the memory care premium to surveyed assisted living rates. They are useful for comparison and unreliable as a quote for any single community — which is exactly why families should ask for the all-in monthly figure, in writing, before deciding.

Methodology

Included: national medians and ranges from the Genworth/CareScout Cost of Care survey as published in public summaries, plus a state matrix compiled from that survey and AARP state data. Excluded: individual facility list prices (they vary by care level and are not a market series), Medicaid reimbursement rates, and any ALFA public release figure — none was verified this session. Figures from different compilations use different survey vintages, so small differences between them are expected.
