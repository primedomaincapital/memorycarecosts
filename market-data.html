What memory care actually costs, state by state

A family comparing memory care communities is usually comparing two different prices at once: the advertised monthly rate and the bill after level-of-care charges. The CareScout 2025 survey puts the national memory care range at $7,440 to $8,060 a month, with a gap of more than $10,000 a month between the cheapest and priciest states. The figures below separate the base rate from the add-ons and show where geography moves the total most.

Assisted living alone shows the same geography effect: the CareScout 2025 survey puts the national median at $6,200 a month, with state medians running from $4,369 in Mississippi to $12,096 in Hawaii. Source: CareScout 2025 Cost of Care Survey, as reproduced by SeniorCareHomes Community fees of $2,000–$5,000 at move-in and level-of-care add-ons as needs increase sit on top of those medians in many contracts. Memory care typically lasts two to three years, and the tail runs to ten. At the national midpoint of $7,750 a month, two years costs $186,000 and three years $279,000 — before community fees and care-level increases.

The honest caveat: CareScout does not publish a separate state-by-state memory care median, so state memory care figures are modeled by applying the memory care premium to surveyed assisted living rates. They are useful for comparison and unreliable as a quote for any single community — which is exactly why families should ask for the all-in monthly figure, in writing, before deciding. State medians hide the variation inside a state — a community in San Francisco or Manhattan can charge double the state median, and a rural community can charge well under it. The median is a planning benchmark, not a quote.

Methodology

Included: national medians and the 50-state table from the CareScout Cost of Care Survey 2025 — fieldwork July to November 2025, results released March 2, 2026, based on 4,944 completed surveys of licensed assisted living communities — as reproduced by SeniorCareHomes and DigitalCare60; the two reproductions agree on every state figure used here. Memory care is modeled, not surveyed: the state assisted living median plus 20% (low) to plus 30% (high), per the National Center for Assisted Living rule; the midpoint (plus 25%) is the single figure used for multi-year totals. Nursing home medians are the surveyed CareScout 2025 national figures: $315 a day semi-private ($9,581 a month) and $355 a day private ($10,798 a month). Excluded: individual facility list prices (they vary by care level and are not a market series), Medicaid reimbursement rates, and any ALFA public release figure — none was verified this session. One reproduction (findseniorlivingnow.com) publishes different state medians for the same survey; five other reproductions agree with the figures used here, so those figures stand. No District of Columbia median is published by CareScout, so no DC row appears.
